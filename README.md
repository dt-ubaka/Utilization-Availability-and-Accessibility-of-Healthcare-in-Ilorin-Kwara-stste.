# 📊 Utilization, Availability and Accessibility of Healthcare in Ilorin, Kwara state.

## 📌 Overview  
This project investigates the impact of the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) on healthcare utilization in Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria. The NHIS was introduced to improve access to healthcare and reduce maternal morbidity and mortality rates.  

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Assess the level of NHIS enrollment and utilization among residents  
- Identify socio-economic factors influencing NHIS usage  
- Examine the correlation between NHIS and healthcare utilization across selected healthcare centers  
- Provide recommendations for improving the scheme’s effectiveness  

## 🧪 Methodology  
- Sample Size: 400 respondents (systematic random sampling)  
- Data Collection: Structured questionnaire  
- Analysis Tool: Excel
- Statistical Methods: Descriptive & inferential statistics

## 📈 Key Findings  
- 73% of respondents were enrolled in NHIS  
- Income, occupation, and education significantly influenced utilization  
- Strong positive correlation between NHIS and healthcare utilization:  
  - UITH: 0.3454  
  - General Hospital: 0.4748  
  - Civil Service Clinic: 0.6786  

## ✅ Recommendations  
- Strengthen healthcare infrastructure  
- Provide continuous training for healthcare workers  
- Establish monitoring & evaluation systems  
- Implement anti-corruption measures to ensure transparency
